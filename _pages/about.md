I am a **Senior Lecturer** at [Karlstad University](https://www.kau.se/en/researchers/mohammad-kakooei) (Geomatics, Department of Environmental and Life Sciences) and a **Postdoctoral Researcher** at the [Institute for Analytical Sociology (IAS)](https://liu.se/en/employee/mohka86), Linköping University.

My research focuses on the intersection of **Machine Learning (ML)**, **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**, and **Earth Observation (EO)**, with applications in disaster management, land cover mapping, urban studies, socio-economic analysis, and sustainable development. I combine advanced AI methods, satellite imagery, cloud computing, and big-data analytics to address real-world environmental and societal challenges.

My work spans post-disaster damage assessment, wildfire monitoring, urban expansion analysis, land cover and wetland mapping, and large-scale socio-economic studies such as poverty and wealth mapping across Africa. I have collaborated with institutions including KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Chalmers University of Technology, Harvard University, and Linköping University.

## Research Interests

- Deep learning for satellite image analysis (multispectral, radar/SAR)
- Poverty estimation and socio-economic mapping from EO data
- Urban and rural settlement mapping at continental scales
- Uncertainty quantification and Bayesian methods in ML
- Time-series analysis of Earth Observation data
- Google Earth Engine, cloud computing, and GPU-accelerated processing
